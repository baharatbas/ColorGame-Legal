Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Water Puzzle: Pour Color (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you play on an iPhone, iPad or Android device. We keep data collection to a minimum.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress (completed levels, gold, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may process certain device and advertising data to serve those ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following information locally on your device so that you can continue where you left off: levels you have completed and your best move counts, your gold balance and purchased levels, scenes you have seen, and your sound and vibration settings. This information is not sent to us and is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data. We configure the App to request non-personalised ads.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App shows a consent form provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. If you do not give consent, ads are not requested. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy settings inside the App, when that option is available in your region.

You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings (on iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads). For more information see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services that identify you.
  • We do not create user profiles and we do not share your information with third parties, except Google AdMob as described above.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

5. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad or otherwise, please contact us and we will help.

6. Data retention and your rights

Because game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights regarding data processed by advertising partners, such as access, deletion or opting out. You can exercise them through the ad settings above or by contacting Google directly.

7. Security

We do not operate servers that store your game data. Network communication in the App is limited to loading ads.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page with its update date.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue on our support page or visit the Support page.
