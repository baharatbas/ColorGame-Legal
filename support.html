Support

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Need help with Water Puzzle: Pour Color? Here are quick answers and the best way to reach us.

Contact us

Please open a support request on GitHub and describe the problem. Include your device model, operating system version and the App version (Settings screen) so we can help faster.

Frequently asked questions

Is the game free?

Yes. The game is free to play and supported by ads. Rewarded ads are optional.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. You can play completely offline. An internet connection is only used to load ads.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. If you uninstall the App or erase its data, progress and gold are removed. There is no cloud account.

I am stuck on a level. What can I do?

Use Undo to take back a move, Restart to begin the level again, or add an extra empty tube. Long-press a tube to pour several matching tubes at once.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings → Ad privacy settings in the App when it is shown for your region. You can also limit ad tracking in your device’s privacy settings.

Which languages are supported?

The App follows your phone’s language automatically and supports 36 languages, with English as the fallback.
